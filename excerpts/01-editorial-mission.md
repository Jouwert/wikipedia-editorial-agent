# Excerpt — the editorial mission, and the line it does not cross

**Scope:** subject selection vs article prose

## Owner-stated mission

> This project exists to increase the visibility and discoverability on Dutch Wikipedia of
> people and groups who are marginalised or structurally underrepresented, including people
> whose public work defends the rights, safety, dignity or participation of marginalised
> communities.

This is stated as a governing project requirement, not a preference, and explicitly not an
agent's interpretation of one.

## The corrective part, stated plainly

The project does not aim only to document the already famous, powerful or institutionally
prominent. It deliberately looks for people whose public significance may be underdocumented
because existing media and Wikipedia coverage reproduce structural inequalities.

A candidate does not need to be among the "most important" people in a field. A compact
biography can be valuable when reliable independent sources support it.

Groups listed as relevant to the search: women, trans and non-binary people; LGBTQIA+ people and
advocates; Black people, people of colour and people with migration backgrounds; disabled people
and disability-rights advocates; people affected by class, housing, migration or institutional
inequality; climate, feminist, anti-racist and human-rights organisers; and people defending
marginalised communities or making overlooked social problems publicly visible.

## The line

> **Advocacy in deciding whom to research is intentional and central to the mission.
> Advocacy in article prose is not allowed.**
>
> Corrective subject selection does not permit promotional prose, fabricated significance,
> unsupported claims or unreliable sourcing. Articles must remain neutral, proportionate and
> verifiable.

## Why both halves are in the same document

It would be easy to publish only the mission statement, and it would misrepresent the project.
The interesting — and defensible — position is holding two normally opposed commitments at once:
a deliberately corrective search, and an uncompromisingly neutral output.

This is also the point most likely to be misread by an agent, which is why the distinction is
written as a governing rule rather than left to judgement in the moment.
